Best Food at Quincy Market

Quincy Market is a great place to hang around with friends and family and enjoy some good food. There are 18 restaurants and 35+ colonnade eateries of all cuisines, so you will surely find something you like.

Many people ask what is the best Bostonian food or what the must-eat is at Quincy Market. The answer is consistent: locals and repeat visitors settle on the same four classics, and most people finish with a combo of lobster roll and clam chowder, followed by a Boston cream pie.

Classic Favorites

  • Clam Chowder
  • Boston cream pie — official dessert of Massachusetts
  • Bostonian baked beans with brown bread
  • Lobster rolls
  • Seafood (lobster, whole belly clams)

Boston Clam Chowder

Boston clam chowder is a cream-based seafood soup that contains clams, diced potatoes, onion and is thickened with crackers instead of flour. Traditional Boston chowder is thicker than other regions' — if you are familiar with clear chowder like the Rhode Island version or the red Manhattan chowder, the Boston cream-based version will take you to another level. Bostonians are obsessive enough to hold a Chowderefest every year in the week of July 4. The two-time winner Boston Chowda Co. serves it in the Quincy Market Food Colonnade.

Boston Cream Pie

Note that Boston cream pie has nothing to do with pies. It is not a pie — it is a cake. A two-layer cake with vanilla custard or cream filling, covered with chocolate sauce. The cake is believed to have been invented at the Parker House Hotel in 1856, where it was originally called "Parker House Chocolate Cream Pie." Over time, different varieties have been developed.

Bostonian Baked Beans and Brown Bread

The Boston brown bread is not the standard loaf of brown bread you are thinking of. Traditionally, it is a slightly sweet steamed bread, sweetened by molasses or golden syrup together with raisins. It is often round because the bread is steamed in a coffee can traditionally. The brown bread is served with homemade baked beans.
