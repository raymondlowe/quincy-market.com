Boston Christmas Markets 2026 — Dates, Locations and Which to Visit

Dates checked 21 November 2026 against the 2026 listing published by each organiser. Opening hours move in the last week before Christmas, so confirm on the organiser's own page before you set out.

Five Christmas markets run in Boston in 2026, and they are not interchangeable. Snowport in the Seaport is the big one: open-air, 125-plus makers, food and bars, free to walk into. The SoWa Christmas Market is a single large room of makers in the South End for $10. The Harvard Square Holiday Fair is a juried craft fair across two December weekends. The Downtown Boston Seasonal Market is two Saturdays on Summer Street, ten minutes from Quincy Market. And the Holiday Market at Quincy Market is inside the building you are already standing in.

Three of the five are indoors, which decides most wet December days. Two are a short walk from Quincy Market; one is across the river.

If you have one Saturday to give a Christmas market: Saturday 12 December 2026. All five are open that day — Snowport from 10:00, the SoWa market from 10:00, the Harvard Square fair from 11:00, the Downtown Boston Seasonal Market 11:00–18:00, and the Holiday Market inside Quincy Market 11:00–19:00. Start at Quincy Market, walk to Summer Street Plaza, then take the 18-minute walk to the Seaport.

Snowport, the Seaport — the Big Open-Air One

Snowport is the market most Boston weekends end up at. It runs in a lot at 100 Seaport Boulevard, next to The Current, with more than 125 makers, food and drink from around the city, custom cocktail bars and live music. General admission is free and there is no ticket, though the queue on a Saturday afternoon in December is real.

It opens on Friday 6 November 2026 and runs seven days a week to Sunday 27 December. It is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday 26 November, and on Christmas Day, Friday 25 December, and it closes early at 18:00 on Christmas Eve.

A weekday before 7 December is the trap: the market does not open until 15:00, so a Tuesday morning in late November has nothing to show you. From 7 December it opens at 11:00 on weekdays, and Saturday and Sunday still open at 10:00.

If you want to skip the weekend line, the Seaport Insider app sells a timed Fast Pass on Saturdays and Sundays only: USD 15 for an adult, USD 20 if bought on the day, USD 7 for a veteran and USD 7 for a child aged 6 to 17, with under-fives free. The general admission line stays free and needs no booking.

SoWa Christmas Market, the South End — the Indoor One

The SoWa Christmas Market fills the SoWa Power Station at 550 Harrison Avenue with more than 100 makers, artists, designers and food producers, all in one room. The organiser calls it Boston's premier indoor holiday shopping experience, and on a raw December day that is the whole argument for it. The organiser bills 2026 as the 11th Annual SoWa Christmas Market.

It opens on Friday 20 November 2026 and runs to Sunday 27 December, with Mondays closed throughout, plus Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

Admission is USD 10 at the door, and the organiser uses it to run the festival — staffing, decoration, entertainment and cleaning. There is on-site parking at 540, 500 and 450 Harrison Avenue and at 274 and 365 Albany Street.

Harvard Square Holiday Fair, Cambridge — Two Weekends in December

The Harvard Square Holiday Fair is the oldest of the five and the smallest in floor space: a juried fair of New England craftspeople and importers, held on the lower level of the Galeria building at the corner of JFK and Winthrop Streets. It has run since 1986 and admission is free.

It is open for two weekends only: Friday 11 to Sunday 13 December, and Friday 18 to Sunday 20 December 2026.

Six days in total, and a Friday at noon is the quietest of them: the aisles are narrow and they fill up on Saturday afternoon. Allow 20 minutes for the fair itself and the rest of the day for the square.

It is the one market on this list you should not walk to. Quincy Market to Harvard Square is 5.8 km, about 70 minutes on foot, and half of it is across the river in the cold. Harvard station sits in Harvard Square, and the Galeria is a two-minute walk from the station exit.

Downtown Boston Seasonal Market — Two Saturdays Close by

The Downtown Boston Alliance runs an open-air winter bazaar on Summer Street Plaza at 10 Summer Street, two blocks from the market hall. It is the closest of the four outside markets and the easiest to fold into a Quincy Market morning: ten minutes on foot, and you are there.

It runs on Saturday 12 December and Saturday 19 December 2026, 11:00–18:00 on both days. Artisan vendors, seasonal wares, no queue culture — it is a plaza market rather than a destination in its own right, which makes it the right stop when you are already downtown rather than the reason to cross town.

The Holiday Market at Quincy Market — the One Inside the Building

Quincy Market's own Christmas market is on the second floor of the hall, so you do not need to leave the building to reach it. It runs in five separate windows between 20 November and 24 December 2026 — 20–22 November, 27–29 November, 4–6 December, 11–13 December, and 18–24 December — every one of them 11:00–19:00.

It is not one continuous run from November to Christmas. A Tuesday in early December gives you the food hall and the shops, and no Christmas market. The window that covers weekdays is the last one, 18–24 December. No admission charge is published for it, and the tree in the marketplace is lit on Saturday 21 November at 18:00.

Which One to Visit

The choice is usually made by the date, the weather and how far you are willing to go — not by which market is "best".

Two combinations work well. Snowport and Quincy Market are about 18 minutes apart on foot, so one morning covers both — market first, then the food hall for lunch. SoWa and Quincy Market are 35 minutes apart on foot through the South End and Downtown Crossing, which is a reasonable December walk in one direction and a short ride back.

What does not work is trying to add Harvard to a Quincy Market day. The fair is 5.8 km away across the river and open only two weekends, so treat it as its own half-day rather than a stop on a downtown loop.

Getting There and Between Them

Quincy Market is the natural base: State, Haymarket and Government Center stations are all within a few minutes, and two of the Christmas markets are inside a 20-minute walk. The full station-by-station directions are on how to get to Quincy Market, and garage rates on Quincy Market parking.

Walking times from the market hall are short enough that you do not need a ticket for three of the five: Summer Street Plaza is 0.9 km, about 11 minutes, and Snowport is 1.5 km, about 18 minutes. The South End is 2.8 km, about 35 minutes, or a short ride. Only Harvard Square needs the T.

Quick Answers

Which Boston Christmas market is closest to Quincy Market?

The Holiday Market on the second floor of Quincy Market itself. Of the markets outside the building, Summer Street Plaza is about 11 minutes on foot and Snowport about 18.

Are Boston's Christmas markets free?

Snowport's general admission is free, as is the Harvard Square Holiday Fair. The SoWa Christmas Market charges USD 10 at the door, and no admission charge is published for the Holiday Market at Quincy Market.

Which Boston Christmas markets are indoors?

The SoWa Christmas Market in the South End, the Harvard Square Holiday Fair in the Galeria building, and the Holiday Market on the second floor of Quincy Market. Snowport and the Downtown Boston Seasonal Market are open-air.

What is open on a Monday in December 2026?

Snowport, which opens at 15:00 on Mondays until 6 December and at 11:00 from 7 December. The SoWa market is closed on Mondays, the Harvard fair runs Friday to Sunday, and the Downtown Boston Seasonal Market runs on two Saturdays. Quincy Market's food hall is open, but its Holiday Market does not run on a Monday until 18–24 December.

When do the 2026 markets start?

Snowport on 6 November, SoWa on 20 November, the Quincy Market Holiday Market with its first window on 20 November, the Harvard Square Holiday Fair on 11 December, and the Downtown Boston Seasonal Market on 12 December.

Can I visit two markets in one day?

Yes — Quincy Market and Snowport are about 18 minutes apart on foot, and Quincy Market and the Downtown Boston Seasonal Market are 10 minutes apart. On Saturday 12 December all five markets are open at the same time.
