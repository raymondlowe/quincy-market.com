Quincy Market — Boston's Historic Food Hall

Quincy Market is the bustling 1826 food hall at the heart of Faneuil Hall Marketplace in downtown Boston. Free to enter, it is home to 35+ colonnade eateries serving classic New England fare — clam chowder in bread bowls, lobster rolls, Boston cream pie, baked beans and brown bread — alongside international stalls, pubs and dessert shops.

This is the place Bostonians send first-time visitors to: a single building, free admission, and an entire meal for under $20. See our Complete Food Guide with Price List 2027 for current prices, the must-eat list, and the full 2026–27 vendor lineup.

Quick facts: Opened 1826 by Mayor Josiah Quincy · 27,000 sq ft · Free admission · Food hall meals $8–18/person · On the Freedom Trail · 3 MBTA T stops within 1 block.
