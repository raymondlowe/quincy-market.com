Quincy Market at Christmas — 2026 Dates, Tree Lighting and What Is Open

Last checked: 15 November 2026. Event dates are the 2026 schedule published by Faneuil Hall Marketplace, the operator of Quincy Market. Hours may change in Christmas week, so confirm at the official site before you travel.

Quincy Market keeps its ordinary hours through December apart from Christmas Day, and on top of them the marketplace runs two dated Christmas events in 2026: a Holiday Market inside the hall over five windows between 20 November and 24 December, and the annual tree lighting on Saturday 21 November. Entry to the hall is free on every one of those days.

The hall's standing weekly schedule is on the Quincy Market hours page.

If you have one date to spare: Saturday 21 November 2026. The Holiday Market opens at 11:00 on the second floor, the tree lighting runs 14:00–18:00 at Quincy Market, and the tree is lit at 18:00. One trip covers both.

The 2026 Holiday Market — Five Windows, All 11:00–19:00

The Holiday Market is a Christmas market held indoors: local vendors selling gifts and seasonal food on the second floor of Quincy Market. It does not run continuously through December. The operator runs it on five separate windows, each open 11:00–19:00 every day it runs.

Four of the five windows are Friday to Sunday only. The fifth is the one that runs straight through from Friday 18 December to Thursday 24 December — the one that covers weekdays at all. Outside those dates the hall is open on its normal schedule, with no Holiday Market in it.

The Tree Lighting — Saturday 21 November 2026, 14:00–18:00

The annual Holiday Tree Lighting takes place at Quincy Market on Saturday 21 November 2026, 14:00–18:00. The four hours are the event: live performances, the marketplace's Christmas decorations, and the switch-on at the end. The tree is lit at 18:00 sharp, with Make-A-Wish and Santa taking part.

That Saturday is also the middle day of the first Holiday Market window, so it is the one date on the 2026 calendar when both Christmas events are running at once. The practical shape of the day: the market from 11:00 on the second floor, then the lighting from 14:00, with the tree lit at 18:00. If the switch-on is the reason you are going, be in place before 18:00 rather than arriving on the hour.

The lighting is a single afternoon. If your Boston weekend falls in December, it has already happened — the Holiday Market windows are what is left in the season.

What Is Open on Which Days

The hall itself does not close for the Christmas events, and the marketplace's standing schedule does not change for them: Monday to Saturday 10:00–21:00, Sunday 11:00–19:00, with the operator's own caveat that holiday, restaurant and retail hours may vary. What changes day to day is the Holiday Market timetable sitting on top of that schedule.

Christmas Week Versus a Normal Week at Quincy Market

On an ordinary week you turn up, walk the colonnade, eat, and leave. In the Christmas period four things are different.

  • The market is not open every day. The Holiday Market exists only inside the five windows. A Tuesday in early December gives you the hall, the food and the shops, but no Christmas market.
  • Weekdays exist only in the last window. Monday 21, Tuesday 22, Wednesday 23 and Thursday 24 December are the only weekdays in the whole season when the Holiday Market is open — four days, all in the final run. If a weekday is the only slot you have, that is the week.
  • The events share one date. Saturday 21 November carries both the market and the tree lighting, so it asks for a longer visit and an evening finish: the tree is lit at 18:00.
  • The market closes two hours before the hall does. On a Friday or Saturday the Holiday Market shuts at 19:00 while the marketplace trades until 21:00, so dinner belongs after the market, not in the middle of it. Our Quincy Market food guide has what to order and what it costs.

The weather changes less than you would expect. The Holiday Market is inside on the second floor, so a wet or freezing December day does not cancel it — the hall's counters and shops are indoors too, with the pushcarts and street performers out on the cobbles. The tree lighting is a single afternoon at a fixed date, so it is the one part of the season you cannot move to another day.

The crowding follows the same logic as any peak day: the hall opens at 10:00 Monday to Saturday and 11:00 on Sunday, the lunch crush runs roughly 11:00–14:00, and arriving at opening is the difference between browsing the colonnade and queueing along it. In the Christmas period add the market crowd on top, and treat a market Saturday as a busy Saturday rather than an ordinary one.

Practical Details

Where the events happen

Both are at Quincy Market — the 1826 granite market hall in the middle of the block. The Holiday Market is on its second floor; the tree lighting is listed at Quincy Market, 14:00–18:00. What else is inside the hall, from the food colonnade to the rotunda, is set out on what is inside Quincy Market.

Getting there

The marketplace stands on the Freedom Trail, a short walk from State, Haymarket and Government Center MBTA stations — the three the operator names. T lines from Logan Airport, walking routes and the station-by-station detail are on how to get to Quincy Market, and garage rates on Quincy Market parking. In December allow for the walk and a few minutes at the door.

Cost

Entry to Quincy Market and the wider Faneuil Hall Marketplace is free: no entry fee is published and no ticket is sold for the marketplace. You pay for what you eat and buy — the shops around the complex are listed on the shopping directory, and the food is on the Quincy Market food guide.

Quick Answers

When is the Quincy Market tree lighting in 2026?

Saturday 21 November 2026, 14:00–18:00, at Quincy Market. The tree is lit at 18:00 sharp.

What are the 2026 Holiday Market dates?

Five windows, all 11:00–19:00, on the second floor of Quincy Market: 20–22 November, 27–29 November, 4–6 December, 11–13 December, and 18–24 December 2026.

Is the Holiday Market indoors?

Yes. It is held inside Quincy Market on the second floor, so rain or snow does not close it.

Is Quincy Market open on Christmas Day?

The operator's last Christmas Day notice, stamped 25 December 2025, says Faneuil Hall Marketplace and Quincy Market are closed on Christmas Day. No 2026 notice had been posted when this page was last checked, so treat Friday 25 December 2026 as closed and confirm on the official site in Christmas week.

Do I need a ticket for the market or the tree lighting?

No. Entry to the marketplace is free and no entry fee is published; you turn up at the listed times.

Which day is quietest in December?

A weekday morning. The hall opens at 10:00 Monday to Saturday, the lunch crowd arrives around 11:00, and the Holiday Market runs on weekdays only from 18 to 24 December. The hours page sets out the weekly schedule and the Sunday caveat.
