Street Performers at Quincy Market & Faneuil Hall — Times, Pitches and Who You'll See

Last updated: October 2026. The performer line-up and daily times change — always confirm on the operator's live calendar before you plan a trip around a specific act.

The street performers play at Quincy Market and the wider Faneuil Hall Marketplace every day from 11:00 to 21:00, the shows are free to watch from the plaza, and there are four fixed pitches — West, South, North and East. Acts come from a fixed roster approved by the marketplace's management team: jugglers, acrobats, magicians, unicyclists, pogo stunt riders, violinists, singer-songwriters and bands. Since 1976 the marketplace has been a professional street-theatre pitch, worked throughout the year rather than only in summer. This page covers when they perform, where each pitch is, who is on the roster, and how to check the day's line-up.

One-line answer: Shows run daily, 11:00–21:00, all year, free to watch, at four named pitches (West, South, North, East). The roster is fixed and approved by Faneuil Hall Marketplace; use the operator's live busker calendar to see who is on and at which pitch on the day you visit.

When Do the Quincy Market Street Performers Perform?

The operator, Faneuil Hall Marketplace, states its entertainment programme runs daily from 11:00 to 21:00, and that the schedule is a live one: individual slots are published each day rather than fixed for the year. So the daily window is the reliable part — be in the plaza between 11:00 and 21:00 and you will almost always catch something — while the specific act and time are rebuilt day by day.

Performances are year-round: the marketplace describes itself as a street-theatre pitch "throughout the entire year", so winter shows are normal. Do not assume a season such as April to October; the operator publishes a daily window, not a season. The authority on any given day is the operator's live busker calendar at faneuilhallbuskers.com, linked from the operator's Street Performers page. It runs on Boston (Eastern) time, switches between day and week views, and filters to Variety or Music; each entry shows the pitch, start time and act.

Where They Perform — The Four Pitches

Rather than roaming at random, approved acts work set pitches around the Faneuil Hall Marketplace plaza — the cobbled open space between the Faneuil Hall building and the Quincy Market food hall. The operator names four of them on its live calendar, each by the side of the plaza it sits on:

The pitches are simply the labelled performance spots on the plaza — no ticketing, no seating and no barrier. You stand and watch, and moving between two acts is a short walk. For the difference between the market building and the wider complex around it, see Quincy Market vs Faneuil Hall.

How the Programme Works

This is a managed programme, not an open free-for-all. Three things are worth knowing.

  • All acts are approved. The operator states plainly that "all street performers are approved by the Management Team and performances are subject to change." You will not see unvetted buskers working the plaza.
  • The line-up can change. The operator also warns that "performance slots may change", which is why a named performer on the roster is not a promise for a particular day.
  • There is a formal route in. Performers apply through two published channels — Variety Entertainment and Music. Variety acts can also apply for a limited Seasonal Guest place each summer, running 1 June to 30 September, which can lead to a full-time roster position. Musicians apply annually, with the deadline on 31 March and decisions notified by 30 April.

If you are a performer reading this rather than a visitor, both application routes are linked from the operator's Street Performers page at faneuilhallmarketplace.com.

Practical Tips for Catching a Show

  • It's free. No ticket, no booking, no charge to watch — you only pay for whatever you eat or buy in the market.
  • Midday to early evening is the core window (11:00–21:00 daily), with the plaza busiest around lunch and through the afternoon.
  • It's outdoors, so weather matters. The programme is year-round, but rain, snow or wind can disrupt a day — check that day's calendar entry before a special trip.
  • Check the calendar, then pick a pitch. Filter by Variety or Music to see the pitch and start time, so you can plan around one act instead of wandering.
  • Pair it with a visit. The plaza is on the Freedom Trail, a short walk from the State, Haymarket and Government Center MBTA stations. See how to get to Quincy Market and the Complete Food Guide with Price List.

Quincy Market Street Performers — Frequently Asked Questions

What time are the street performers at Quincy Market?

Entertainment runs every day from 11:00 to 21:00, year-round, as published by the operator. The slot for each individual act changes daily, so use the operator's live calendar at faneuilhallbuskers.com for the day you plan to visit.

Are the street performers free to watch?

Yes. The shows happen on the marketplace plaza, and the plaza is free to enter — there is no ticket and no booking required. You walk up and watch, and you only pay for food or shopping.

Where do the street performers perform?

At four fixed pitches on the Faneuil Hall Marketplace plaza, which the operator labels West, South, North and East. Each daily slot on the calendar is listed against one of these pitches.

Do they perform in winter or in the rain?

The programme runs throughout the entire year, so winter shows are normal. Because the pitches are outdoors, heavy rain or a storm can affect a day's schedule — the calendar is kept in Boston (Eastern) time and is the best check.

Can anyone perform at Quincy Market?

No. All performers are approved by the Faneuil Hall Marketplace management team, and the roster is fixed. Performers apply through the published Variety Entertainment or Music routes (plus a limited Seasonal Guest window for variety acts from 1 June to 30 September).

Whatever is on when you visit, the shape of the experience is the same: a working street-theatre pitch running since 1976, four named spots around the plaza, and a free show on the cobbles between the food hall and Faneuil Hall.
